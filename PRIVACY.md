# Ro5n3R Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Ro5n3R turns Chrome's new-tab page into a personal launchpad. This policy describes the information the extension uses and where it goes.

## Information kept in your Chrome profile

Ro5n3R saves your settings, display name, weather city, pinned shortcuts, hidden site suggestions, tasks, captures, schedule, timer progress, and optional resized background picture in `chrome.storage.local`. This information stays in your Chrome profile. Ro5n3R has no developer-operated server or account, and the developer does not receive this information.

You can change or remove saved items in the extension. Chrome also controls extension storage, including what happens when you remove the extension or clear its data.

## Optional browser information

If you choose to enable the corresponding feature, Ro5n3R reads your most-visited sites, recent browsing history, or bookmarks from Chrome to show them on the new-tab page. These permissions are optional and can be turned off in the extension's settings. Ro5n3R does not copy those Chrome lists into its saved data or send them to the developer or another service. Site icons come from Chrome's local favicon cache.

## Weather requests

If you enter a city and the Weather widget is shown, Ro5n3R sends the city name to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. It then sends the returned coordinates and your selected temperature unit to Open-Meteo's forecast service. These requests use HTTPS. As with other internet requests, Open-Meteo may receive network information such as your IP address. Ro5n3R does not send your history, bookmarks, tasks, or other saved content to Open-Meteo. Weather requests occur when the new-tab page opens with a city set or when you change the city or units; there is no background polling.

## Search and links

When you submit a search, Chrome opens your selected search engine (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, or Brave) with your query. When you open a shortcut, bookmark, or recent page, Chrome connects to that site. Ro5n3R does not send these queries or links to the developer.

Ro5n3R does not use analytics or advertising and does not sell user data. Its use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use requirements.

For questions about this policy, use the [Ro5n3R GitHub repository](https://github.com/perzival-22/Ro5n3R/issues).
